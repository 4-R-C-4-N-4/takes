Chess with armies you design.

Win by checkmating the enemy king. Except for en passant, a capturing piece always lands on the square it takes.

Start quickly

Choose Vanilla on Play for familiar chess. Select your colour and an Easy, Medium or Hard CPU, then press Play. The CPU runs in your browser. Hotseat lets two people play on one device.

Build an army

  • Open Builder. Fill any of your three starting ranks; the bottom row is your back rank and the top row is rank 3.
  • Spend at most 39 points. Unspent points are allowed. Costs are experimental and may change in future ruleset versions.
  • Place exactly one king, on your back rank. The Builder checks king exposure and other errors before saving.
  • Queen, Coroner (BN), Hierophant (RN) and Bane (RBN) are each capped at one per army.
  • Tap a palette entry, then tap squares for repeated placement. Tap the entry again to deselect. Dragging works too.
  • Save your army, then select it on Play. Open Library to inspect each piece's moves and attacks.

Read the shorthand

New pieces use movement notation first; their names are secondary nicknames. B3N means diagonal slides up to three squares, plus knight jumps.

CodeMovement
R / B / NRook / bishop / knight
R2 / B3Rook movement up to two squares / bishop movement up to three
W / FOne square orthogonally / diagonally
D / AJump exactly two squares orthogonally / diagonally, over the adjacent square
f / v / sForward-only / vertical-only / sideways-only modifier on the following movement
RBNRook, bishop and knight movement combined (Bane)
fR3fB3Up to three squares forward, straight or diagonally (Triton)

Sliders cannot pass through pieces; jumpers can. Forward is relative to the owner. All current officers mirror left/right and forward/backward. Current non-classic pieces capture where they can move; the standard pawn keeps its usual distinction.

Castling and promotion

Castling uses the king and its eligible rook-tier partner, fixed at game start, ending on the usual castled files. The Builder identifies your partners. The normal clear-path and king-safety conditions still apply.

All pawn-tier pieces promote on reaching the far rank. Choices come from non-pawn pieces in your own starting army; if there are none, the fallback is Queen. Prince (WF) is an ordinary capturable piece, not a second royal king.

Play a friend online

  1. The host chooses an army and presses Create game. In this first release, the host plays White.
  2. Share the invite link privately. Your friend opens it, chooses an army and joins as Black. They can also enter the invite code on Play.
  3. Both armies are committed before either is revealed. Once connected, play normally.

Each browser saves its own session locally. Reload the same game URL in the same browser to resume. Keep the host tab open; don't open a duplicate of the same seat in another tab.

Privacy and connection limits

Public Nostr relays exchange connection details; moves use an encrypted WebRTC data channel. Public STUN services help establish the connection. Relays see connection metadata, and WebRTC may expose your public IP address to your opponent. Treat the invite code as a shared secret.

There is no TURN fallback yet: some networks block direct connections. Public relays can also be unavailable. Try another network if connecting fails. This is casual P2P play, without accounts, ratings or trusted server adjudication. A disconnect is not proof of a win or loss.

Sessions are saved only on your devices, not in the cloud. Clearing site data or losing both copies can lose the game. Saved armies keep their original ruleset version.
